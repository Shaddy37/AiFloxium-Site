# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Operations leaders and founders at small-to-mid-size B2B services companies (agencies, consultancies, professional services) drowning in repetitive workflows across HubSpot, Notion, Slack, Airtable, and Sheets. They are time-poor, hire-skeptical, and have been burned by AI vendors who sold demos that never shipped. They want a single senior operator to audit their stack, design a fix, and ship a working system in 7-14 days — not a six-month engagement, not a course, not a "framework".

## Product Purpose

AIFLOXIUM is a one-person AI automation studio operated by Muhammad Shadab Shams. The brand exists to convert cold traffic into a discovery call by proving, in the design of the site itself, that the studio ships rigorous, opinionated, technical work. Success = booked qualified calls and a steady pipeline of $5K-$25K build engagements, NOT passive "thought leadership" impressions.

The site is the product. Every section is a working demonstration of the studio's voice, taste, and operational discipline. Hero video runs the brand register; the trust strip names real stacks; the pricing section commits to a number; the process section counts to four; the contact section is a calendar link. There are no ebooks, no courses, no "join the waitlist" CTAs. One funnel: visitor → call booked.

## Brand Personality

**Three words:** direct, rigorous, unhidden.

**Voice:** Short. Specific. Operator-grade. No hedging, no "we empower", no metaphors. The founder is named on the page, on the LinkedIn, on the X, on the email signature. The brand argues against AI hype by refusing to sound like it.

**Emotional goals:**
- Confidence: the visitor trusts that the studio can actually deliver.
- Calm: the visitor is not pressured; the offer is clear, the next step is obvious, and there is one of it.
- Respect: the visitor's time and intelligence are not insulted by recycled vendor copy.

## Anti-references

What AIFLOXIUM is NOT:
- Not a 2024-25 Vercel-deployed AI agency: no Geist+Geist Mono default fonts, no glassmorphism as a default surface, no bg-clip-text gradient on every heading, no `border 1px + drop-shadow` ghost cards.
- Not an "AI transformation" consultancy: no "supercharge / empower / unleash / leverage / 10x", no aphoristic cadence, no "X theater" copy.
- Not a SaaS-template landing page: no "01 — About / 02 — Process / 03 — Pricing" scaffolding on every section, no rainbow-palette eyebrow, no permanent-marker brush accent, no 8 simultaneous autoplay videos.
- Not anonymous: no "our team of experts", no stock photography, no unverified testimonials.
- Not a content site: no blog-with-no-CTAs, no "subscribe to our newsletter" lead magnet, no gated whitepaper download.

## Design Principles

1. **Specificity beats hype.** Every claim names a tool, a duration, a number, or a verifiable outcome. "Wire HubSpot → Notion → Slack in 7 days" beats "AI-powered workflow automation".
2. **One decision per section.** One primary CTA, one heading color, one motion register, one eyebrow pattern (used sparingly), one card style. Section overload is the AI grammar we refuse.
3. **The site is the portfolio.** Every component on the page is a working example of the studio's craft: type rhythm, motion discipline, contrast, mobile behavior, hover affordances, content density. The studio uses the same rules for client work.
4. **Operator-grade defaults.** Permanent link to a real calendar. Real email, real LinkedIn, real X. No "DM to inquire". No chatbot. No popups on first scroll.
5. **Dark because of context, not because it looks cool.** The site targets late-night builders, post-call operators, and US/EU evenings (PK timezone is +5h). Dark surfaces reduce eye strain and signal "we work when you do".

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 body contrast, 3:1 large-text contrast, keyboard navigation on every interactive element, visible focus rings, `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` honored on every animation, mobile menu with focus trap + Escape close + aria-expanded.
- Reduced motion: full text + state visible without animation; Lenis disabled; Framer Motion animations short-circuit to final state.
- Multilingual roadmap: English-only at launch, `dir="ltr"` set explicitly, `lang="en"` on `<html>`, content is short enough to translate sentence-by-sentence.
- No autoplay video without poster; no audio without a visible control.
