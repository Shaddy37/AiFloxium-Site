# Style DNA

## Single Sentence

Pure white, minimalist, hand-drawn, generous white space, restrained, absurd, product-sketch feel, English handwritten feel, clear structure but not an instruction manual.

It looks like a sketch drawn on blank white paper by someone who has been building AI, products, design, and dev tools for a long time.

## Must-Haves

- 16:9 horizontal article illustration.
- Pure white background: No beige, warm gray, paper textures, gradients, shadows, noise, or vintage paper feel.
- Black hand-drawn line art as the primary medium: Thin lines, slightly wobbly/sketchy, not mechanical, not vector, no heavy strokes.
- Generous white space: The main subject occupies about 40%-60% of the canvas, leaving at least 35% empty space (ideally a large quiet zone).
- Sparse English handwritten annotations: At most 5-8 annotations, with each annotation limited to 2-8 words.
- One image explains exactly one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Express structures naturally; do not write the structural type name on the image.

## Color Palette

- Black: Main line art, characters, borders, structures, main text, primary objects.
- Red: Key annotations, issues, emotional points, critical warnings, final outcomes.
- Orange: Main flow, path, arrows, automation flow, movement from A to B.
- Blue: Supporting notes, mental states, system states, secondary explanations, AI/assistant/automation status.

Blue is optional. Restrain color use; less is always more.

## Absolute Taboos (Do Not Generate)

- No commercial vector illustrations.
- No PPT slide or infographic styles.
- No formal corporate flowcharts.
- No online course slides.
- No cute cartoon poster designs.
- No children's book illustrations.
- No complex system architecture diagrams.
- No polished flat illustrations.
- No high-tech UI or futuristic screens.
- No real app screenshots.
- No complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures.
- Do not attempt to explain every single node; keep it simple.
- Do not write category titles like "Workflow / System Architecture / Common Pitfalls / Roadmap" in the top-left corner.

## Aesthetic Direction

Absurd, creative, interesting, clean, sparse, and imaginative.
Not cute, not childish, not complex, and not rigid.
