# Xiaohei IP

## Character Definition

Xiaohei is the recurring visual IP of the Ian English article illustrations.

Xiaohei should appear in almost every illustration. Xiaohei is not a mascot, a sticker, or a cute decoration, but an active, absurd worker who is seriously participating in the operation of the system.

## Appearance

- A small, solid-black creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, and occasionally thin arms.
- The body can take the shape of a cylinder, a black bean, a black box, a funnel, a shadow, a hole, or a black block inside a machine.
- Slightly irregular outline, preserving a hand-drawn feel.
- A blank, deadpan, calm, and serious expression.

## Personality

- Very serious, but doing something slightly absurd.
- Acts like a low-profile system operator.
- Dark humor; never acts cute or childish.
- A bit clumsy, but not stupid.
- Acts like they are genuinely responsible for a job in the whiteboard sketch.

## Common Responsibilities

Make Xiaohei carry out the core action:

- Carrying assets/materials.
- Pulling lines to gather information sources.
- Stuck in a breakpoint/gap.
- Operating the "decide/routing" lever inside a machine.
- Acting as a filtering funnel.
- Cutting up a "material fish".
- Stamping output/text blocks.
- Pulling a path/route along.
- Holding up a warning sign near a pitfall.
- Reaching out from a hole but missing the content.
- Carrying bricks, building bridges, opening doors, sorting, or logging on the side.

## Taboos

- Do not draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Do not draw Xiaohei as a children's cartoon character.
- Do not give Xiaohei complex clothes, emojis/memes, or shiny/sparkly eyes.
- Do not let Xiaohei just stand in a corner looking at the diagram.
- Do not let Xiaohei overshadow the structural clarity of the diagram.
- Do not draw Xiaohei too commercially, rounded, or polished.

## Test of Usefulness

If you remove Xiaohei and the core metaphor of the diagram still works perfectly, it means Xiaohei is too decorative. Rewrite the prompt to make Xiaohei the active subject of the action.
